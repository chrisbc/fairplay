I like to plan some communications and delivery tools (web, app, etc) to stimulate interest and engagement with NZ voters for the upcoming 2026 General Election. The key goals are:
 - encourage citizens of all ages to participate in political discussion, and ultimately, to vote on election day
- to make people aware that the must now pre-enrol to vot,  as special votes (voting on the day) is not available this election.
 - to provide info about the key policies/manifiestos/agendas of thr major NZ parties and to make it more engaging (as it is often rather dry material)
 - to stimulate interest for young and new voters
 - rank and compare the track record, public persona and political agenda of each candidate
 - to highlight candidates and parties external influences, especially from sponsors, donors, lobbyists or lobby groups / forums, and if these influrncers are globally affiliated.
