## Project Goals

1. Encourage NZ citizens to participate in political discussion and vote
2. Raise awareness about pre-enrolment requirements (special votes potentially unavailable)
3. Make party policies/manifestos more engaging and accessible
4. Stimulate interest among young and new voters
5. Rank and compare candidate track records and political agendas
6. Highlight external influences on candidates/parties (donors, lobbyists, global affiliations)

## Key Concepts

### Platform: "The Ballot Box NZ" / "The Public Office"

A Progressive Web App (PWA) combining:
- **Pre-enrolment tools** with urgency messaging
- **Policy matcher** (Tinder-style quiz to match voters with parties)
- **Candidate scorecards** with voting records and public sentiment
- **Influence mapping** showing donor/lobbyist connections
- **Submission portal** for voter feedback on bills

### International Models Researched

- **Germany: Wahl-O-Mat** - Government-backed VAA (21M users in 2021)
- **Netherlands: StemWijzer** - Simple 3-option policy matcher
- **UK: TheyWorkForYou** - MP voting records and accountability (20+ years running)
- **US: BallotReady** - Local election guides with "cheat sheet" approach

### Proposed Tech Stack

- Frontend: React/Vue.js (PWA)
- Backend: Python/Django
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Data Sources: Electoral Commission, Hansard, Register of Pecuniary Interests

- Non-partisan credibility (Charitable Trust/Society structure)
- Simple UX with visual results
- Media partnerships for reach
- Transparency in data sourcing
- Mobile-first design
