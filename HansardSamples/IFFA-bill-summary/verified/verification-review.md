## Clarifications

### 1. Levy vs Rates Priority

**Analysis states:** "Rates rank first in sale proceeds → Equal ranking - proportional distribution if insufficient"

**Clarification:** The Bill inserts section 75C into the Local Government (Rating) Act 2002. Section 75B retains a detailed priority order for distributing proceeds, but section 75C applies when proceeds are insufficient to cover both rates and levies at certain priority levels - in that case, distribution is proportional to outstanding amounts.

**Citation:** Bill lines 4832, 4858 (new section 75C); Bill line 109 (explanatory note).
