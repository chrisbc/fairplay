The Perverse Incentive Structure

Incentive to keep stranded assets running

SPV investors may lobby to keep infrastructure operating even when alternatives are cheaper — because a mothballed asset with a running levy is politically toxic.

Incentive against alternative investment

While the levy runs, investing in alternatives does not reduce it. The levy makes transitions more expensive, slowing the very changes it was meant to enable.

Incentive to overstate risks

Proponents have every incentive to overstate risks to justify compulsory levy funding — especially with no affordability assessment, no consultation, and no council veto.
