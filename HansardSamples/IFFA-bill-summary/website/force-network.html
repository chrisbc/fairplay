The Force Network

Who shapes the rules, who makes the sole decision, and who bears every cost for 30 years — mapped as a three-tier power structure with all removed safeguards shown.

⬇ Bears the Cost — Low Power, High Exposure
🏠 Homeowners Compulsory levy · No consultation · No affordability check · Forced sale risk after 4 months non-payment
🔑 Renters Costs passed through by landlords · No voice · No protection · Regressive burden
🌿 Māori Landowners Consent bypassed for large projects (5,000+ properties) · Treaty interests unassessed
